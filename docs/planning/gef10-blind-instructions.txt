BLIND LABEL CHECK -- instructions for ONE labeller (work alone; another labeller does the same pack).

Your pack is the directory PACK (given by the parent). Use ONLY these files:
  PACK/reference.png    12 monster species, each under a LETTER (A-L), each drawn in SEVERAL
                        views side by side (directions and animation frames). No names.
  PACK/grids/grid_NN.png  the crops, 20 per grid, each under its number (#001 ...).
  PACK/crops/NNN.png    the same crops one by one, enlarged (open one when a grid tile is too small).
  PACK/pack.json        the list of crops and the three questions.
Never open anything outside PACK (no answer key, no other directory, no repository file, no
other labeller's answers). Do not guess from file names or order: they carry no class information.

Every crop shows a GREEN rectangle drawn 1 px just OUTSIDE a label box. For EVERY crop answer:
  Q1: is there a monster inside the green box?           yes / no / unsure
  Q2: the box:  good  (tight around the monster)
                loose (the monster is in it, with much background)
                off   (cuts the monster or misses it)
                n/a   (when Q1 is no)
  Q3: which species is it?  one LETTER from reference.png, or none / unsure
The game scene is an open field with grass, dirt paths, orc huts, wooden fences and large trees;
parts of it lie in shadow under tree canopies, and sprites may be partly hidden by leaves,
trunks, bushes or fences. Monsters may stand next to other monsters or next to the player's own
character (a red-haired rogue), and some are FIGHTING that character: they are then drawn in an
attack pose (swinging a weapon) or flinching, and may overlap the character. Judge the box
against the monster's whole drawn body and weapon, not against the character beside it. Some
species look alike: several are orcs of the same skin colour that differ mainly in SIZE and gear,
and some differ mainly in COLOUR: compare size, gear and colours with reference.png, not only the
shape. Judge the box against the ONE monster it is drawn around.

Write ANSWERS (the path the parent gives you) as a JSON list, one object per crop, every crop:
  [{"crop": "001", "q1": "yes", "q2": "good", "q3": "B", "note": ""}, ...]
"note" is free text (what you saw when unsure, a second monster, a UI window, ...).
